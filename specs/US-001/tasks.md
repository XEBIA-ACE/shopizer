# Tasks: US-001

- [ ] T001 FR-US-001-AC1: The HRMS v1.0 requirements document SHALL contain an explicit statement in the scope and/or feature sections that multi-currency employee self-service checkout is out of scope for the HRMS v1.0 release, such that a reader can clearly identify that this capability will not be delivered in v1.0.
- [ ] T002 FR-US-001-AC2: The functional requirements sections that describe employee profiles, leave, performance, and payroll for HRMS v1.0 SHALL NOT include any user journeys or flows that involve a cart, any product or service selection for purchase, or any monetary checkout, so that payment-related journeys are not implied within HRMS v1.0.
- [ ] T003 FR-US-001-AC3: The non-functional requirements for HRMS v1.0 (including performance, availability, and security targets) SHALL NOT reference, rely on, or assume the existence of any checkout process or multi-currency payment capability, and any such references, if present, SHALL be removed or re-scoped from the v1.0 baseline.
- [ ] T004 FR-US-001-AC4: Where future transactional payment needs are documented in the HRMS requirements, those needs SHALL be captured as separate future initiatives and/or epics outside the HRMS v1.0 scope and SHALL NOT be listed as part of the HRMS v1.0 release scope; if the documentation structure makes this separation ambiguous, the requirement owner SHALL add explicit wording clarifying that such payments are out of scope for v1.0.
- [ ] T005 FR-US-001-AC5: The requirements management process for HRMS v1.0 SHALL obtain and record sign-off from compliance, security, and audit stakeholders confirming that the exclusion of multi-currency checkout for HRMS v1.0 is clearly documented in the approved requirements baseline.
